KillaAssistant

EpiGuard

EpiGuard

Dengue early-warning platform predicting risk up to 4 weeks ahead for Colombia's 32 departments. Six model families were benchmarked on 2022–2023 held-out data — SARIMAX was deployed over the slightly better SARIMA for one reason: it attributes each forecast to specific climate variables, making predictions interpretable for public-health decision-makers. Integrated SIVIGILA surveillance, NASA POWER meteorology and NOAA's ONI; weighted RMSE of 25.28 across four horizons.

Granger

Full-stack project management app on MERN + end-to-end TypeScript. Beyond the Kanban UI, the real work is in the auth surface: refresh-token rotation with reuse detection (a stolen token revokes the entire chain), httpOnly + SameSite=None cookies tuned for the Vercel/Render cross-site split, and 4 rate-limit buckets backed by Upstash Redis. SugarcaneLeaf

SugarcaneLeaf

Foliar disease segmentation for sugarcane. A YOLO26m-seg model trained on 6,000+ field images (5 disease classes) achieving mAP@0.5 of 91.7%, exported to LiteRT and served entirely in the browser via WebGPU — no server roundtrip for inference, p99 of 47ms client-side. The FastAPI backend exists only to collect latency from every visitor into a ring buffer — observable MLOps, with a live p50/p99 dashboard and automatic WASM fallback on unsupported browsers.
